# Full Stack Overview

## Mission

The DOP-AI ecosystem is designed as a layered operational architecture supporting resilience, transparency, environmental protection, operational intelligence, and public visibility.

Built under the foundational doctrine:

> Dignity Over Power

The ecosystem is modular, interoperable, and prevention-focused.

- transparency requirements

Constitutional governance layer.

## Layer 2 — Topology

Repository:
- systems-map

Purpose:
Define:
- ecosystem structure
- interoperability
- operational pathways
- deployment relationships

Role:
Architectural coordination layer.

---

## Layer 3 — Operational Intelligence

Repository:
- phoenix-intel-grid

Purpose:
Transform telemetry into operational awareness.

Core systems:
- telemetry ingestion
- anomaly detection
- escalation systems
- geospatial intelligence
- operational dashboards

Role:
Operational intelligence layer.

---

## Layer 4 — Environmental Infrastructure

Repository:
- trv-shield

Purpose:
Protect environmental and public systems through prevention-first infrastructure.

Core systems:

- equalization systems
- modular treatment
- flood survivability
- environmental telemetry

Role:
Infrastructure protection layer.

- resilience over fragility
