# Office of Citizen

## Public Operational Visibility & Civic Intelligence

Office of Citizen is the public interaction and transparency layer of the DOP-AI ecosystem.

Built under the foundational doctrine:

> Dignity Over Power

The platform is designed to provide human-readable operational visibility across civic, environmental, infrastructure, and resilience systems.

Office of Citizen prioritizes:

- transparency
- public awareness
- operational clarity
- civic participation
- accountability
- resilience visibility

---

# Mission

To improve public understanding and operational visibility before instability escalates into widespread human harm.

The platform exists to support:

- public operational dashboards
- environmental visibility
- infrastructure awareness
- civic transparency
- escalation communication
- resilience coordination
